Good to know

Questions & policies

Everything clients usually ask before they book. Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How long will my project take?

Usually 5–7 working days for a Standard logo, about 2 weeks for Normal and 3–4 weeks for Max. Pro timelines are agreed together. Exact dates are confirmed before you pay.

How does payment work?

A 50% advance by UPI starts the project. The balance is paid before the final files are delivered. Bank transfer is available on request.

Can I get a refund?

If we haven't started work, your advance is refunded in full. Once concept work has started the advance is non-refundable, but we keep refining within your package's revisions until you're happy.

How many revisions do I get?

Standard 2 rounds, Normal 5, Max unlimited, Pro unlimited with a dedicated manager.

Which files will I receive?

Print- and web-ready PNG, JPG, PDF and SVG files, plus your colour codes and the fonts used.

Who owns the final design?

You do. Full rights transfer to you after the final payment.

Do you work with clients outside Kerala?

Yes. Everything happens online through WhatsApp and email.

Can I order just one poster or menu?

Yes. Choose “Single job” in the brief and we'll send you a quote.
